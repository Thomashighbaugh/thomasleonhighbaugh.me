---
title: "Privacy Policy"
date: "11/17/2026"
---

This site does not track you. There is no analytics product installed here, no advertising, no tracking pixel, no comment system, no newsletter, and no account to sign into. Nothing you read is attached to a profile, because there is no profile and no database holding one. The whole site is a set of static files.

## What stays on your machine

Search runs in your browser against an index that ships with the site, so the words you type never reach a server. The fonts are self-hosted, so loading a page makes no request to Google Fonts or any other font CDN. The scripts on the page draw stars and meteors, or move the scroll position.

No page here sets a cookie. Your light or dark preference is stored in your browser's local storage and never leaves it.

## What Vercel sees

The site is served by Vercel. Any host has to receive the request it answers, which means your IP address, your user agent, and the URL you asked for. That processing happens on Vercel's side under [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy), and their policy governs it, not mine. I have no database of those requests and no way to look you up in one.

The one measurement enabled here also belongs to Vercel: Speed Insights. It reports anonymous performance data, meaning the route and URL, a Web Vital and the element responsible for it, plus coarse browser, operating system, device type, connection speed, and country. [Vercel's documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/speed-insights/privacy-policy) states that this data is not tied to an IP address or an individual visitor and cannot be used to reconstruct a session across pages. Nothing of mine is attached to it, and I cannot identify a reader from it.

## Links out

Posts link elsewhere. Once you follow a link, the site you land on decides what it collects and its policy applies, not this one. That includes my resume, my links page, and every repository and documentation page I point at.

## Changes

If any of this stops being true, this page will say so and the date above will change. If I ever add analytics, a comment system, or anything else that collects something, it gets described here before it goes live.
